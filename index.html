01 — Profile

A practitioner of systems, not slogans.

I've spent my career inside the engine rooms of global organizations — engineering, oil & gas, and for the past thirteen years, biopharma — building the learning architectures that allow strategy to actually happen. Capability is the rate-limiting step of every transformation, and most enterprises underinvest in it by design.

My work sits at the intersection of learning science, organizational design, and digital innovation. I've launched academies serving thousands of employees across sixteen international markets, introduced VR, AI roleplay, and gamified learning inside regulated environments, and led change programs where the measure of success was business outcomes, not training hours.

Trilingual, pragmatic, and quietly obsessive about the details that separate theatre from transformation.

02 — Practice

Where I do my best work.

I.

Enterprise Learning Architecture

Federated operating models that pair central standards with local flexibility. Competency frameworks, academy design, governance, and the unglamorous plumbing that makes scale possible.

II.

AI-Era Capability Building

Enterprise-wide AI upskilling programs for tens of thousands of employees. Vendor strategy, budget architecture, behavioral learning design, and the champion networks that carry adoption.

III.

Change Leadership

Prosci-grounded change execution for transformations that cross functions, geographies, and comfort zones. The white space between departments is where most change dies — I work there.

IV.

Digital Learning Innovation

VR, AR, AI-assisted roleplay, gamified ecosystems, and XP economies deployed inside regulated industries. Applied learning science, not shiny objects.
